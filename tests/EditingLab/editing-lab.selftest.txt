# Spoken edits (docs/specs/voice-commands.md, "Editing what you typed"). Run with tests/EditingLab/run.ps1.
stoponfail
listen
say: hey jarvis
expecttitle: Edit Lab
expectfocus: Message
say: start typing
say: Hello there, my good friend and family.
expectvalue: Hello there, my good friend and family.
say: delete the last two words
expectvalue: Hello there, my good friend
say: and colleagues.
expectvalue: Hello there, my good friend and colleagues.
say: The review is on Monday at noon.
say: replace the word Monday with Tuesday
expectvalue: Hello there, my good friend and colleagues. The review is on Tuesday at noon.
say: delete the word noon
expectvalue: Hello there, my good friend and colleagues. The review is on Tuesday at.
say: delete the last sentence
expectvalue: Hello there, my good friend and colleagues.
say: scratch that
say: stop typing
